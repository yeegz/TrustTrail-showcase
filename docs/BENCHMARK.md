## Read this before any number below

**These numbers are regression evidence only. They are NOT a measure of real-world accuracy.**

- Every fixture is **synthetic and fictional**: invented names, phone numbers, account numbers, shops and links. None of it is a real conversation.
- The fixtures were **written by the same author as the rules**, and the rules were then adjusted until the fixtures passed. A perfect or near-perfect score here says only that the engine still does what its author intended. It says nothing about how often a real seller's wording is caught or wrongly flagged.
- **No native speaker has reviewed** the English or the Malay items, the informal Malay or the code-switched Manglish. Real usage will differ in ways the author could not anticipate.
- No real marketplace data, no independent labelling and no held-out set were used.
- For those reasons the analysis tier for **English and Malay is BETA** (`ANALYSIS_BETA`, `ANALYSIS_BETA`), and it stays BETA until an independent, natively reviewed evaluation exists. Every other language is `ANALYSIS_UNSUPPORTED`: stored and shown, not analysed.
- The Wilson intervals describe sampling uncertainty **within this synthetic set**. They are not confidence intervals for field performance.

## History

- v1, 2026-11-11: on the first run against the engine as first written, 84 of the 840 cases disagreed with it (56 false positives, 28 false negatives). Examples: "I'm online at night" read as a request to move to the Line app, "Comes with remote control" read as a remote-access request, "Boss nama apa?" read as a third-party payee, "Hantar gambar IC awak" not read as an ID request, and any amount followed by a word ("RM 1,650 nett") dropped. The pattern tables were then corrected until the set passed; no fixture expectation was changed. That is exactly why the 100% figures above are not evidence of real-world accuracy.

## Known limits of this benchmark

- Precision and recall are near their ceiling because the same person wrote both sides. Treat any drop as a regression to investigate, and any real-world claim as unsupported.
- 15 to 19 items per cell is small: even a clean cell has a Wilson lower bound for precision of only about 80%.
- Several rule families (links, repeated reviews, contradictions) depend little on language; their "ms" cells mostly re-test the same logic with Malay context words.
- The set has no audio, no real screenshots and no OCR noise beyond a few planted misreads.
